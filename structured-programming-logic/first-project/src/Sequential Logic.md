\# Sequential Logic



Sequential logic is one of the fundamental ways of representing the execution of instructions in a program. It follows a sequence of steps, where each instruction is executed in the order in which it was defined, one after another.



It is an important foundation for understanding how computers execute algorithms and how programs work.



\## Example: Baking a Cake Following a Recipe



1\. Gather all the necessary ingredients.

2\. Put the eggs, milk, flour, and sugar into a bowl.

3\. Mix the ingredients until the batter becomes smooth.

4\. Pour the batter into a baking pan.

5\. Place the pan in the oven.

6\. Wait for the cake to bake.

7\. Remove the cake from the oven.



\## Main Idea



A computer executes instructions by following a logical and sequential order. Each step must be performed according to the established sequence to achieve the expected result.
